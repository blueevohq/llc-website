Privacy policy

Your privacy, in plain English

We collect only what we need to build your website and run our business. We never sell your information.

Last updated November 16, 2026

1. Who we are

This policy covers Blue Evolution Interactive, LLC ("we", "us") and our website, blueevolutioninteractive.com. Questions go to hello@blueevolutioninteractive.com.

2. What we collect

  • What you send us: when you request a preview or email us, we get your name, business name, email or phone, links to your listings, and your message.
  • Your business's public info: to build a preview, we use information your business already shows publicly, like reviews, photos, hours and contact details from Google, Yelp, Facebook or Instagram.
  • Payment details: payments are handled by Stripe. We see your name, email and what you paid for, but never your full card number.
  • Basic technical data: like most websites, our hosting provider may log standard information such as your IP address and browser type to keep the site running and secure.

3. How we use it

  • To build your free preview and your website
  • To reply to you, send updates, and handle billing
  • To keep our records and meet legal and tax requirements

We don't use your information for advertising, and we never sell or rent it.

4. Who we share it with

Only the services that help us run the business, and only what they need:

  • Stripe for payments and billing
  • Our email provider for sending and receiving email
  • Website hosting and form providers that run our site and deliver form messages

We may also share information if the law requires it.

5. Cookies

Our site doesn't use advertising or tracking cookies. It may remember your language choice (English or Spanish) in your browser so the site shows the right one next time.

6. How long we keep it

We keep your information while you're a client or while we're talking about a project, and afterward only as long as needed for records, taxes or legal reasons.

7. Your choices

  • Ask to see, correct or delete the information we have about you
  • Reply "no thanks" to any email from us and we'll stop contacting you

Just email hello@blueevolutioninteractive.com.

8. Children

Our services are for businesses. We don't knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

9. Changes

If we update this policy, we'll change the date at the top. For clients, we'll mention any major changes by email.
