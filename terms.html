3. What's included

The setup fee turns your preview into a finished, live site. The monthly fee keeps it running and up to date.

Setup includes:

  • Your changes to the preview: wording, photos, colors and services
  • Registering or connecting your domain, with secure https
  • Your contact form sending requests to your email
  • English and Spanish versions
  • Pointing your Google, Yelp and Facebook listings to the new site
  • Final checks on phones and computers before launch

The monthly plan includes:

  • Hosting and your domain renewal
  • Small edits, done within 2 business days of your request
  • A real person to text or email for changes
What counts as a small edit: updating content that's already on your site, like text, prices, hours or promotions, or swapping up to 5 photos per request.

Quoted separately: new pages or sections, a redesign, online booking or payments, and extra languages. We'll always tell you the price before starting.

4. Ownership and canceling

You always own your domain and your content. If you cancel, you keep both.

  • Your domain: registered in your business's name. If you leave, we'll help transfer it to you or your new provider at no charge. After that, renewing it is your responsibility.
  • Your content: your logo, photos, reviews and text stay yours. You confirm you have the right to use anything you send us.
  • To cancel: email or text us, or cancel through your billing link. Canceling takes effect at the end of the current paid month. After that, hosting stops and the site goes offline.
  • Keeping the site files: if you'd like the site's design files to host elsewhere, you can buy them for a one-time fee of $250. Otherwise the design stays with us.
